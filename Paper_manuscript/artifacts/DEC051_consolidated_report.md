# DEC-051 — H3/H4 verification campaign: consolidated report

Branch `review/h3-h4-verification-dec051` from `c07c482`. Plant C2t_Vsafe_R35_040, original B2 RDC, nothing in the plant, gains, graph or controllers changed. The attached protocol file was not provided as a file; the text of the
authorization was the specification. H3 and H4 are the REFORMULATED hypotheses (H3-R, H4-R); the original HOCBF / CLF-HOCBF hypotheses are not claimed. No paper work.
Budget: 6 of 6 dynamic simulations (2 contingency: one 2000 s state-dump run crashed at t = 1999 s because of an attribute error in the dump code and was repeated; 2 H3; 2 H4). Everything else is static or post-processing.

## Findings that change earlier results (disclosure)
1. **The case carries an active PSS.** IEEEST: KS = -2 on electrical power, lag 0.75 s, washout 4.2 s. The E9-A linearisation (used by E13, E9-H, DEC-050) dropped all 42 IEEEST states, i.e. it removed the PSS feedback. The blocks F1, F2, LL1 have all time constants zero and are represented by 5 forced states + 2 forced algebraic variables per SG; they are eliminated exactly here and the two genuine PSS states per SG are kept (N = 208 dynamic states + 20 references).
2. **The analytic (ANDES-based) Jacobian assembly used since E9 has entry errors of up to 1.7 % in some columns** (Pset rate rows vs. BusFreq states; Frobenius 0.13 %). Because the slowest mode depends on the capability factor of SG31 (g_Q = 0.12), its time constant moves from 504 s (E9 machinery) to **427 s** (central-difference Jacobian of the nonlinear field), which is what the simulations show (417-419 s). All DEC-051 stability statements use the numerical Jacobian. The DEC-050 C3 modal numbers (428 s, min damping 0.073) are superseded: slowest 427 s, min damping 0.072 at the exact equilibrium.
3. The DEC-050 B2_QV1 and frontier results are unaffected (they are simulation results).

## A. Exact equilibrium (GATE A: EQUILIBRIUM_EXACT)
Full DAE + 20 references + original RDC rates solved simultaneously (damped Newton, inner re-solve of algebraic variables and actuator maps at every iterate, angle pinned at SG31) from the 1999 s ANDES state.
Because all rotor angles are states, the object is a **relative equilibrium**: all angle rates equal one common value (1.25e-6 rad/s, i.e. a 2.0e-7 Hz synchronous offset set by the RDC leak) and every other rate is zero. Quotient residual 5.1e-12 (declared target 1e-8); algebraic residual g 6e-14; RDC rates 5e-12 pu/s; PSS output ~1e-17. Two starts perturbed by 1e-3 relative converge to the same root (|dx| <= 7.8e-10, |du| <= 1.8e-9). Distance to the 1999 s state: 1.8e-3 (x), 1.9e-3 (u). Algebraic block regular (min singular value 2.1e-3). All unit powers inside limits, 39 buses 0.978-1.041 pu, SG z-clamp margin 0.087, min capability factors g_P 0.119 / g_Q 0.133. Reproducibility of the re-run: reference vector within 3.0e-6 pu of DEC-050.
Existence is **CONDITIONAL**: Newton-Kantorovich needs a second-derivative bound <= 1.5e3 (scaled norm); sampled ratios reach 160; not verified as a bound.

## B. Local stability (H4-R)
* **H4-1 (PROVEN, computer-assisted).** Quotient Jacobian (227 states, common angle shift removed; exactly one zero direction in the full matrix, none in the quotient): max Re(lambda) = -2.34e-3 /s, no non-negative real part, min damping 0.072 (1.30 Hz), slowest modes 427, 304, 141 s. A-posteriori Lyapunov certificate: P (A^T P + P A = -I, scaled coordinates) is symmetric positive definite (lambda_min 5.7e-4, solver error ~1e-8) and ||A^T P + P A + I||_2 = 2.3e-5 (+ evaluation bound 1e-2) < 1, which implies Hurwitz independently of the eigenvalue accuracy. Jacobian step study (h vs 10h): Frobenius 1.9e-9; entrywise perturbations of 1e-4 move the abscissa by < 2e-6. (The single-zero-direction test originally coded as a singular-value ratio failed because of stiffness (sigma_max 1.2e4); it was replaced by the eigenvalue count; the original value is recorded in the JSON.)
* **H4-2 (PROVEN, computer-assisted).** Lyapunov's indirect method on the quotient reduced field: relative equilibrium exact, algebraic block regular (implicit function theorem), field C^1 where no limiter/clamp/positive part switches (142 elements, all inactive, smallest margin 0.087), Hurwitz Jacobian => local exponential stability modulo the common rotor-angle shift. Scope: this equilibrium, neighbourhood of **unquantified** radius. Floating point, no interval arithmetic.
* **H4-3: REGION_NOT_CERTIFIED.** No validated bound on second derivatives. Empirical: dV/dt < 0 in all 81 sampled directions (including the 5 worst-case directions of the quadratic form) up to the largest tested level rho = 4.6e-3, but P/Q/S limits are lost above rho ~ 5e-5 (margin +0.078 pu at 4.6e-5, -0.15 pu at 4.6e-4). The state immediately after the Q+ step (pre-event differential state) has V = 9.2e2, i.e. 2e5 times outside the stability sampling and 2e7 times outside the physical-limits sampling.
* **Time-domain validation (2 runs, 1000 s, +/- 0.02 pu along the slow mode of the E9 Jacobian).** Converge to the equilibrium; V decreases monotonically; V(1000)/V(0) = 0.0083 / 0.0089 against the linear prediction 0.0086 (all-time error <= 3 %); observed decay of the reference error 417 / 419 s vs 427 s predicted; min physical margin +0.11 pu throughout. (A first prediction with the E9 Jacobian, 504 s, was 115 % off at 1000 s: that discrepancy is how the Jacobian error was found.)

## C. Preventive reserve (H3-R)
* **Instant jump** (algebraic solve with all differential states continuous; matches the first simulated sample within 2.3e-3 pu for all units). P+ 58.564 MW: SG31 jump +0.087 pu (C2t) to +0.089 pu; SG31 reserve 0.040 pu (C2t) -> 0.139 pu (dispatch of the B3-A solution): **C2t violates SG31 at t = 0+ (post-jump margin -0.047 pu) before any controller can act**; the references have authority <= 1.5e-3 pu per pu of reference step at 4 ms and <= 0.04 at 50 ms (E9-A).
* **Attribution.** Voltage set-points alone: SG31 -0.003 pu (no effect). Dispatch alone: +0.099 pu. Both: +0.096 pu. SG35 is not hit by the jump (+0.022 pu) but by the slow electromechanical excursion (peak at ~7.8 s, +0.062 pu); the R35 redispatch raises its reserve 0.070 -> 0.110 pu and the observed minimum margin goes -0.014 -> +0.025 pu.
* **Inequality** R_i >= dP_i^jump + max(fast, slow increment) + eps (eps = 2.3e-3 pu): holds for C2t_Vsafe_R35_040 and fails exactly where simulations violate (C2t SG31, Vsafe SG35). Bound classes: jump = conservative algebraic computation on an amplitude grid (monotone on the grid, not proved in the continuum); fast/slow increments = empirical, scaled linearly with the amplitude; no universal bound.
* **Frontier prediction.** The inequality crosses zero at **70.3 MW**, inside the DEC-050 dynamic bracket 65.9-73.2 MW.
* **Independent validation (predictions fixed in `IEEE39_DEC051_H3_predictions.json` before the runs).** H3-1, P+ 110 % (64.42 MW, bus 4): predicted margins SG31/SG35 0.0137/0.0160, observed 0.0137/0.0160, max error over the ten units 3e-4 pu, no violation. H3-2, P step 58.564 MW at bus 18 (selected analytically as the smallest positive predicted margin among admissible buses; the first selection rule picked a 58.6 MW load at SG31's own terminal, a trivial violator, and was replaced before any simulation; the admissibility test also dropped the upper voltage bound because the pre-event case already has buses above 1.05 pu): predicted 0.0768/0.0118, observed 0.0777/0.0230 (conservative), max error 0.06 pu on a unit with margin > 4 pu, no violation. Eight buses are predicted to violate with 58.564 MW (31, 23, 21, 24, 16, 7, 15, 8): analytic prediction only, not simulated.
* **GATE C: H3_PREVENTIVE_NUMERICALLY_SUPPORTED.** Not CERTIFIED: the transient increments are empirical. Q+ (17.345 MVAr): all post-jump P/Q/S margins positive (min P margin 0.121 pu) and the historical runs show no crossing.

## D. Joint statement
No joint guarantee. Domains: regularity (sampled), local stability (exists, unquantified), physical limits (sampled to rho = 5e-5), post-jump states (far outside). The certified/empirical regions are around the Q+ post-event equilibrium only; the P+ post-event equilibrium was not computed; nothing is extrapolated to the jump or to other disturbances.
