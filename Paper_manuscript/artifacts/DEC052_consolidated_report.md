## 1. Findings that change earlier statements
1. **The 1.7 % Jacobian discrepancy was a DEC-051 harness defect, not an E9 error.** `e9h_predictor.build()` evaluates the RDC at `st['pset'], st['qset']`; DEC-051's `load_eq()` left them at the pre-event values (error up to 0.249 pu in Qset), so the capability factors g_P, g_Q were wrong. With the equilibrium references the analytic and central-difference Jacobians agree to 1.3e-7 and both give τ = 426.854 s, ζ_min = 0.07213 (stale: 504.0 s, 0.0815). The DEC-051 sentence attributing entry errors to the E9-A/E9-H/E13/DEC-050 assembly is **withdrawn**; the numerical Jacobian used for all DEC-051 stability numbers was correct. E13 and DEC-050 use live references, so only the omission of the PSS states remains as their defect, and it is small at the equilibrium (τ 426.89 vs 426.85 s; ζ_min 0.0734 vs 0.0721).
2. **The H3 voltage inconsistency (1.07 vs 1.0444 pu) came from screening the wrong plant.** The DEC-051 screen used the historical C2t case (max 1.0730 pu, 21 buses above 1.05 pu); the simulations ran C2t_Vsafe_R35_040 (0.9794–1.0444 pu). With the original two-sided band on the right plant, all 19 candidate load buses are admissible and bus 18 is still the selected scenario.
3. **The reserve prediction is optimistic at non-binding units when the location changes.** In the bus-18 run the bus-4 empirical excursion is exceeded at SG39 (−0.0600 pu, 26 ε) and GFM32 (−0.0366 pu, 16 ε); SG31/SG35 stay conservative. ε covers only the jump term.
4. **Wording:** "PROVEN (computer-assisted)" for H4-1/H4-2 is withdrawn (see §3). The DEC-051 log date (2026-10-28) is one day after the commit and the clock (2026-10-27); a correction note is appended, the entry is not edited. DEC-049 has no entry and none was invented.

## 4. Answers to the mandatory questions
1. **Rigorous certificate of the DAE–RDC root?** No. ROOT_NUMERICALLY_VERIFIED only; needs L ≤ 3.05e3 on a ball of radius 1.2e-8 (scaled), which requires an interval enclosure of J(X) that cannot be built here.
2. **Does the reduced model reproduce the PSS and physics faithfully?** Yes within the audited domain (GATE A passed): exact symbolic elimination of the degenerate chain, 12 genuine PSS states kept, PSS→exciter and te→PSS connections confirmed, symmetry reduction exact, agreement with the full-ANDES 1000 s runs to ≤ 1.6 %.
3. **Validated bound of the Jacobian error?** No. Only consistency numbers (1.3e-7 relative between two derivations; 8.7e-6 to 3.6e-4 absolute in balanced coordinates between finite-difference steps), which are not bounds.
4. **Exact Jacobian rigorously certified Hurwitz?** No. The stored computed matrix is (validated FP bounds); the exact one is certified only conditionally on δ_A < 2.06e-4 (balanced) – 1.2e-7 in the DEC-051 scaling.
5. **Local nonlinear exponential stability without pending conditions?** No. CONDITIONALLY_ESTABLISHED / NUMERICALLY_SUPPORTED_LOCAL_STABILITY, conditional on the existence of the root near z0 and on the Jacobian tolerance; radius unquantified; no global, regional or finite-step claim.
6. **Certified safe invariant region?** No (REGION_NOT_CERTIFIED).
7. **H3 scenario selection within the original voltage limits?** Yes on the simulated plant (all candidates and both runs inside 0.95–1.05 pu); DEC-051 had screened the wrong plant and relaxed the upper bound unnecessarily; selection unchanged.
8. **Accuracy of the reserve inequality for all units?** Binding units SG31/SG35: exact or conservative in both runs (errors +4e-5/+2e-5 and +8.5e-4/+1.1e-2). All units: H3-1 max 3.3e-4 pu; H3-2 max 0.0600 pu with optimistic direction at SG39 (−0.0600) and GFM32 (−0.0366), non-binding. ε = 2.3e-3 covers only the jump; the inequality is not a universal guarantee.
9. **Results to substitute or reinterpret?** See `IEEE39_DEC052_superseded_results.csv`: the E9/E13/DEC-050 Jacobian-error attribution (invalidated), the 504 s figure, the "PROVEN" wording of DEC-051, the H3-2 voltage relaxation, the DEC-051 log date; E9-A/E13/DEC-050 modal numbers become diagnostic with the DEC-051/DEC-052 values (426.854 s, ζ 0.0721) as reference.
10. **What may enter the IEEE paper?** See §5.

## 5. Material admissible for the future manuscript (not written)
* **Lemma (symmetry reduction).** Quotient by the common angle shift: Qm S = I, M v = 0, eig(M) = eig(A_q) ∪ {0}; supports relative equilibria with nonzero common rate. (Algebraic argument + numerical invariance check.)
* **Proposition (conditional local stability).** If a relative equilibrium ξ* exists, the algebraic block is regular and the field is C¹ near ξ*, and the exact quotient Jacobian lies within 2.06e-4 (balanced 2-norm) of the computed one, then the equilibrium is locally exponentially stable modulo the angle shift. State it as conditional, computer-assisted with validated floating-point bounds for the computed matrix.
* **Algebraic-jump computation and attribution** (SG31 improvement comes from the dispatch, not from the voltage set-points; C2t violates SG31 at t = 0⁺ before any control action): numerical result.
* **Reserve inequality as a design criterion/predictor**, with the explicit split algebraic term / empirical terms, the frontier 70.3 MW within the dynamic bracket 65.9–73.2 MW, and the two independent validations including the non-conservative units. Not a guarantee.
* **Experiments:** the 1000 s ± runs (V ratio 0.0083/0.0089 vs 0.0086 linear), 427 s mode, ζ_min 0.072.
* **Not admissible:** rigorous existence of the equilibrium, rigorous Hurwitz of the exact Jacobian, rigorous nonlinear local stability, any invariant region, universal or location-independent reserve guarantee, original H3 (HOCBF) and H4 (CLF-HOCBF).
