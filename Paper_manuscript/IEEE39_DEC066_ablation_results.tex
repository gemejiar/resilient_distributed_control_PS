\emph{Design level.} The five designs differ by 2--7~MW of redispatch (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_designs}). The dense 0--50~ms evaluation changes the active constraints at few unit--event pairs, mainly SG31 for the bus-8 events and SG33 for the bus-23 events (largest gain $\Delta E_{dense}=0.017$~pu); it costs 2.25~MW (B versus A). The overshoot term $\varepsilon_{OS}$ moves the design by 0.17~MW and the predicted margin by $4\times10^{-4}$~pu (C versus B), and the set of active constraints is the same in A--E. The band $U_{D8}$ accounts for most of the extra redispatch of the full design (3.5~MW alone, 4.4~MW on top of C). Design B has a KKT residual of 0.598 (the others 0.06) and is not a converged solution; A was seeded with M0 in addition to M3.

\emph{Observed effect (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_obs}, Figs.~\ref{fig:f4} and~\ref{fig:f7}).} The first lot was the event with the largest predicted contrast, bus 8, severity S3, which is an active constraint of every design. A violates the Pmax of SG31 by 0.0148~pu for about 1.5~ms (class UNSAFE); E, which adds the same band $U_{D8}$ as the full design but without the dense evaluation, violates it by 0.0038~pu (inside the band, INDETERMINATE); B and C keep a positive margin (0.0023 and 0.0027~pu, INDETERMINATE); and D keeps 0.0136~pu (SAFE). The margin of the limiting unit is reached 2.2~ms after the event in all five cases. The same ordering appears at bus 21, S3 (A: $-0.0110$, B: $-0.0072$, E: $-0.0094$, D: $+0.0041$~pu), bus 15, S3 (A: $-0.0068$, D: $+0.0082$~pu) and bus 23, S3 (A: $+0.0006$, D: $+0.0156$~pu). In total, M3 (A) violated Pmax in 4 of the 6 events in which it was run (buses 8, 21 and 15 at S3 and bus 31 at S2), whereas the full M3+OS design (D) violated it only at bus 31, S2, where all designs are inadmissible at the algebraic jump (observed $-0.128$~pu for A and $-0.099$~pu for D) and the improvement is a reduction of the excess and of its duration (58~ms to 37~ms), not a restoration of admissibility. Under the strict class rule, only A is UNSAFE outside bus 31 (buses 8 and 21 at S3); the other violations lie inside the numerical band $\pm U_{D8}$ and are reported as such. Bus 21, S2 is a control in which both designs are safe, as predicted ($+0.091$ and $+0.106$~pu). The frequency nadir (59.965~Hz at bus 8, S3) and the minimum bus voltage (0.970~pu) are the same for the five designs to within $10^{-5}$~Hz and $10^{-4}$~pu, so the designs differ in the sub-cycle power peak and not in the system-wide response.

\emph{Attribution.} (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_contrasts}.) At bus 8, S3, the dense evaluation adds $+0.0171$~pu of observed margin (B versus A) and removes the violation; $\varepsilon_{OS}$ adds $+0.0004$~pu (C versus B), which is below the declared numerical band and is a null result; and $U_{D8}$ adds $+0.0109$~pu, which is by construction the size of the band. The uniform band alone (E versus A, $+0.0109$~pu) does not remove the violation at bus 8 or 21, whereas the dense evaluation under the same band does (D versus E, $+0.0174$ and $+0.0134$~pu) for 2.4~MW more redispatch. The advantage of the full design over a uniform increase of the margin therefore comes from the temporal refinement, not from $\varepsilon_{OS}$. The contrasts are not additive and a factorial design was not run; the full-design effect cannot be split further with these data.

\emph{Prediction quality.} Over the 15 cells that are not inadmissible at the jump, the observed margin exceeds the predicted one by $4.3\times10^{-4}$~pu on average (maximum $2.4\times10^{-3}$~pu at bus 23, S3, D, where the observed limiting unit is SG33 and the predicted one is GFM36); all differences are positive, i.e. conservative (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_obs}). In the two inadmissible cells the error is $1.6\times10^{-4}$~pu. The step sensitivity of the bus-8 peak was tested in three runs at $\Delta t/2$ (designs B and E) and $\Delta t/4$ (design E) with the local refinement kept: the minimum margin changed from $+0.00228$ to $+0.00219$~pu (B) and from $-0.00384$ to $-0.00394$ and $-0.00397$~pu (E), a change of at most $1.3\times10^{-4}$~pu with unchanged sign and class, inside the band $U_{D8}$.

\emph{Scope of this evidence.} The events with the largest contrast, buses 8 and 23, are active constraints of the design and are therefore design-set events (in-sample) for all variants; their selection from the predictions is disclosed and the result shows that the predicted constraint is reproduced, not that it generalises. Buses 21 and 15 were not design events (bus 21 is one of the blind buses of the earlier study, so its use here is retrospective), but they are two cells and one operating condition. Design B was not converged, the initialisation of A differs, and the five designs are not a factorial. The effect is a few percent of Pmax, it acts within the first 3~ms, and its relevance for protection or equipment depends on a temporal tolerance of the limit that is not known; the results are RMS/phasor and do not support any statement about converter-level behaviour.
